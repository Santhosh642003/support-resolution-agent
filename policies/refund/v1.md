SIMULATED BUSINESS. This is a fictional policy for a synthetic company. It is not real.

1. Refund window: 30 days from delivery date, or from the presumed-lost date if never delivered.
2. Lost or undelivered shipments: full refund of the shipment value, including the shipping charge paid for that shipment. The shipping charge is refundable only when the entire shipment is lost or undelivered; it is not refundable for a partial or item-level refund where other items arrived.
3. Auto-approve ceiling: 7500 cents, applied to the cumulative total refunded against the order, prior refunds included. Above that, human review.
4. Partially consumed subscription boxes: the unshipped share is refunded, prorated by item count (items not yet shipped out of items in the box), rounded up to the next cent in the customer's favour and never more than the amount paid for the line.
5. Digital add-ons: non-refundable once accessed.
6. Items flagged final_sale: non-refundable regardless of window.
