SIMULATED BUSINESS. ARCHIVED: superseded on 2026-03-01 by Refund Policy v2 and Cancellation Policy v2. Kept for reference only; do not rely on it.

1. How long do I have to ask for a refund? You have 30 days after your order arrives, or after it is declared lost.
2. What if my package never arrives? We refund the full value of a shipment that is lost or undelivered.
3. Are some refunds approved automatically? Refunds up to 75 dollars are approved automatically; larger refunds are reviewed by a person.
4. Can I get a refund on a subscription box I have already partly used? Yes, it is prorated by the number of items shipped.
5. Can I get my money back for a digital add-on? Not once you have opened or accessed it.
6. Can I return a final sale item? No. Final sale items cannot be refunded whatever the window.
7. Can I cancel my subscription at any time? Yes, and the cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
8. Will I be refunded for the current billing period? No, the current period is not refunded.
9. I am on an annual plan. Do I get money back if I cancel? Within your first 60 days you receive a prorated refund of unused whole months, minus a 10 percent administrative fee.
10. Is there a cooling-off period? No, there is no cooling-off period.
11. How long does shipping take? Standard delivery takes 7 business days.
12. When is a package considered lost? If it has not arrived 10 business days after it shipped.
